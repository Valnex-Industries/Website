# Transcribed specification tables

Every table in this first part is transcribed from the rejoined catalogue
spreads in `public/catalog/spec-sheets/`; the second part, "From the New
Catalogue 2025", covers the machines the spreads do not. Figures are copied as printed, including the
catalogue's own inconsistencies of spacing and case (`VI 02A` has a space, the
rest do not; `VAL- 15HP` is printed that way).

Rows that run past the bottom edge of the scan are marked. Nothing below a cut
is transcribed, because nothing below a cut is legible.

The site reduces these matrices to ranges, because `ProductSpec` is a flat
label/value pair and a product page is not the place for a 14-column table. The
full matrix stays reachable from the product page as the spec-sheet image.

### Irregularities in the 2025 data

Kept as printed above, and kept off the site where they would mislead:

- **Water Cooled Chiller `VI040W`** prints `—` for cooling capacity and
  connected load, and `43` for compressor kW where its neighbours print a
  per-compressor figure (`18.1 / 2`). The site quotes no compressor figure for
  the water-cooled series; its capacity range (2.7 – 65.5 TR) is unaffected.
- **Dehumidifier** — the 23 °C / 60 % RH extraction row rates the `VI 5DH`
  (250 L/day) below the `VI 3DH` (300 L/day), the reverse of every other row.
  The site quotes only the 30 °C / 80 % RH rating. "Humidity Control: Up to
  40% - Digital 25%" is ambiguous and is not quoted.
- **Volumetric Feeder** — the gram-per-second column does not match the kg/h
  column for `VAL 5` (0.5 kg/h is 0.14 g/s, not 1.1). The site quotes kg/h
  only. `A`–`F` are dimensions keyed to a drawing that is not in the file.
- **Hopper Loader** — the column order (`VAL-85` before `VAL-12`) is the
  catalogue's own.
